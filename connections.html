Context: Why CUSTOMER_ID is Vendor-Specific

Data architecture note: In Komet Sales, CUSTOMER_ID is unique per vendor -- it is the vendor's internal customer identifier. The same real-world buyer has different CUSTOMER_IDs at different vendors. This means "buyer buys online from other vendors" analysis requires a cross-vendor identity resolution layer (e.g., matching on buyer company name, email, or location) that does not exist natively in Snowflake.

The "352 never-online pairs" from the deck likely used such a resolution. The K2K_CONNECTIONS table provides the best proxy for cross-vendor pair identification. Trust level: ASSUMPTION -- the 352 figure needs validation against the original methodology.
