Operating rule

Every agent output must update one surface and one ledger row.

TX agent ownership comes from Koronet_OS/agents/mercurio and Koronet_OS/agents/socrates, plus the TX strategy canon. Other local infrastructure can support narrow tasks, but it must not replace Mercurio or Socrates. Current Koronet handoffs live in /Users/facu/Koronet_OS/handoffs/. Feedback/call ingestion is refreshed by python3 scripts/tx_sync_feedback_pipeline.py.
