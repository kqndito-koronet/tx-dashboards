Weekly cadence

What happens each week

Monday leadership prefill

Executive read, OKR pacing, initiative blockers, strategic account escalations, decisions needed.

Tuesday/Wednesday build and route

Rose refreshes data, Mercury updates account actions, Socrates drafts owner asks, Pablito tracks missing inputs.

Thursday Friday-pack lock

Owners edit judgment, learnings, blockers, expectations. They do not rebuild context.

Friday execution review

80% accounts/actions/results/learnings/blockers; dashboards only if they change an action.
