Operating principle

Improve the package and the system at the same time.

Package

Make the strategy, initiatives, Friday rows, leads, and account asks clearer for stakeholders.

System

Make the routines, workflows, feedback loops, call ingestion, and reporting less dependent on manual chat.

Safety

Do not upgrade anything to active status without owner validation, Rose evidence, or Facu approval where required.
