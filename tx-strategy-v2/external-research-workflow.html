External Research Workflow

External research is a Koronet workflow owned by Mercurio. It helps understand a wholesaler, its public ecommerce experience, catalog, market, customers, and competitors. It is not internal truth until reconciled and reviewed.

Back to cockpit TX agent handoffs Agent runbook Compliance

When to use

Use this before account strategy when public context matters.

Account context

Website, ecommerce path, visible catalog, branches, service area, customer type, social/review signals.

Competitor context

Nearby sellers, public online experience, product/categories, delivery/terms signals, differentiation.

Strategy input

Questions to validate with the client, not final account priority or client-facing claims.
