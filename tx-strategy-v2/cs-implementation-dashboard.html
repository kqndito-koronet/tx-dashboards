Metric Decision Flow

For each account, ask the same questions every week.

1. BUYAre they buying digitally?

Procurement purchases, K2K/Procurement connections, approved connections, first and repeat purchases.

2. LISTIs useful supply online?

Bunches, boxes, varieties, future availability, in-transit, and online supply similar to offline business.

3. SELL / UsageAre buyers transacting online?

Online sales, active buyers, first/second/repeat orders, AOV/frequency, campaign/source when Growth starts.

If a metric is not moving, the owner adds why: no connection, no product usage, no supply, no buyer activation, pricing/offline habit, product blocker, or no customer willingness.
