BUY / Connections

Turn connections into repeat transactions.

BUY owns the question: after a buyer and supplier are connected, what makes the relationship actually transact, repeat, and create trust online?

Back to cockpitFriday prefillLead BValue-prop gate

What this is

Not “create connections.” Make connections useful enough to repeat.

Customer value

Help buyers source better, find the right supplier, and make repeat procurement easier.

Koronet learning

Learn why connected pairs do or do not buy again: supply, price, trust, habit, ops, terms, or timing.

Friday output

Pairs worked, first/second/third order movement, blocker type, next action, owner, due date.
