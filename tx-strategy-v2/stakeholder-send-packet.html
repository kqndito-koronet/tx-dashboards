Short version

Team,

Sharing the TX Enablement V2 owner-validation package for Friday.

The goal is simple: learn what it takes to move more floral trade online.

Short term, we can still capture value from top accounts, API/pricing, billing integrity, and rate work. That matters. But durable TX growth depends on growing GMV, digitalizing more of that GMV, and reducing leakage back to calls, texts, manual sourcing, and offline sales motions.

For Friday, we are using BUY -> LIST -> SELL -> GROW as the execution frame:

BUY: turn connected buyer/supplier pairs into repeat transactions.
LIST: make the wholesaler's real sellable supply visible and trustworthy online.
SELL: make implementations become real BUY/LIST/SELL usage.
GROW: amplify accounts that are ready and diagnose what blocks the ones that stall.

What I need from each owner is not a rebuilt deck.
