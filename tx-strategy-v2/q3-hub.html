Q3 Focus

Use existing customers and products to learn what it takes to digitalize more of the market.

BUYBuy more digitally

What do wholesalers need to buy, why do they not buy online, and what would make it repeatable?

LISTPublish real supply

Bunches, varieties, future availability, in-transit, and online supply similar to what they sell offline.

Commercial ReadinessUse Koronet deeply enough

Implementation should help accounts use products, buy digitally, list supply, and reach first online sales.

GROWScale only when ready

Growth Program is the upside for accounts with enough usage, supply, buyer signal, and willingness.

Product enablers support this only when tied to an account blocker, pilot, KPI, and learning loop.

What CS + Implementation work on now

Not six initiatives. Two operating jobs plus a Growth gate.

CS + ImplementationHelp wholesalers buy digitally

CS + ImplementationHelp supply become sellable online
