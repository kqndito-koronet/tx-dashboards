What this is for

Use the hub to ask for execution and learning without making owners rebuild the story.

VisibilityMake good execution visible

Show who is working the right accounts, testing the right plays, and bringing useful learnings back.

ExpectationsAsk for specific work

Each owner should know which accounts/lists matter, what action we want, and what result or learning would count.

HelpReduce stakeholder lift

The system should prefill evidence, context, calls, SFDC activity, product usage, suggested next actions, and talking points.

Current read

Short term, monetize what exists. Q3, learn what it takes to digitalize more of the market.

TodayWe can still take quick wins

Top accounts, API, pricing, billing integrity, and better online buying can move near-term revenue.

ButThat is not durable enough

GMV is not growing enough yet. Fee growth eventually needs more GMV and more digital GMV.

FridayThe work is execution + learning

Run plays with what exists, learn what blocks digital buying/listing/selling, and escalate only what needs product or leadership.

RuleProduct scales what works

Do not build every idea first. Test plays, measure them, then use product to scale the patterns that prove real.
