Review rule

Strategic account plans separate known facts from proposed next moves.

Known

Only sourced status or Facu-confirmed facts.

  • Confirmed strategic account
  • Current owner lane
  • Known blocker or active work

Unknown

Questions that should be answered before broad execution.

  • Current BUY/LIST/SELL behavior
  • Owner willingness
  • Best first conversation

Next move

One action that reduces ambiguity.

  • Conversation to schedule
  • Data pull to request
  • Decision Facu/Cata must make
