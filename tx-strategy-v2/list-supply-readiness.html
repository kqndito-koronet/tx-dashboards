LIST / Online Supply Readiness

Make the wholesaler's real sellable supply visible online.

LIST owns the question: does online supply represent what the wholesaler actually sells, at the formats and time horizons buyers need, with enough confidence to transact?

Scope

Not just bunch uploads. Supply readiness is six dimensions.

Format

Boxes, bunches, units, stems: what buyers actually want to buy.

Variety

Does online reflect what the wholesaler actually sells offline?

Time depth

Today, tomorrow, this week, next month, in-transit, and future availability.

Freshness

Upload/update consistency so supply does not decay.

Fulfillment confidence

Inventory accuracy, substitutions, cutoffs, delivery constraints.

Buyer base readiness

Enough online buyers or reachable clients to make supply useful.
