eCommerce and Offline Are Different Purchase Motions

Why don't offline buyers just switch to online? Because they're buying different things.
Critical insight: the "channel" IS the product
eCommerce = pre-order channel. Buyers order full cases (Boxes) from import/farm shipments. They know what they want, order in advance, receive packed product. This is a planning purchase.

Offline = walk-in/call-in channel. Buyers see loose stems (Units) in the cooler, pick what looks good, buy individual bunches. This is an impulse/availability purchase.

Implication: "Moving buyers from offline to online" doesn't mean the same buyer types the same order on a website. It means enabling the walk-in/call-in buyer to see and order loose inventory digitally. That requires listing Units (the live cooler) online — not just having the Box catalog on an eShop.
