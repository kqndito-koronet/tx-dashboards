If a pair survives the first 2 months, high probability of staying. The intervention window is month 1. Push for 3+ orders in the first month (63.4% retention vs 35.3% for 1-order pairs). Blitz connections retain better than Handshake (66.8% vs 55.7%).

Program Design Note
