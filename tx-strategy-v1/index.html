What we are trying to learn

How do we digitalize more of the market?

Where we are

GMV is nearly flat. Digital GMV is growing in some areas, but most fee growth has come from monetizing what was already there.

Short-term job

Take quick wins where they exist: top accounts, API, pricing, deal desk, and helping customers buy better online.

Strategic job

Use every initiative to learn what would make more buying, listing, and selling happen online, then scale what works.

What we believe

Fees become durable when more GMV can become digital and fee-bearing.

Long term, durable fees require: Grow GMV -> Digitalize GMV -> Monetize digital GMV.

Short term, we can monetize existing value and quick wins, but that does not solve the strategic problem.

The strategic work is learning why the market is not more digital today and what would help customers BUY, LIST, SELL, and then GROW online.

Wholesaler-to-retailer is the core battleground. Wholesaler buying helps first, but wholesalers cannot sell online what they do not list online.

Consistent tracking

Every update must separate GMV, Digital GMV, Monetization, and Learning.

This prevents a good monetization quarter from hiding whether we are actually growing the digital pie.
