What we are trying to learn

How do we digitalize more of the market?

Where we are

GMV is nearly flat. Digital GMV is growing in some areas, but most fee growth has come from monetizing what was already there.

Short-term job

Take quick wins where they exist: top accounts, API, pricing, deal desk, and helping customers buy better online.

Strategic job

Use every initiative to learn what would make more buying, listing, and selling happen online, then scale what works.

Value proposition

The customer story must support the operating strategy.

Client promise

Grow your floral business: sell more, source smarter, and run leaner on the network the trade already runs on.

Wholesaler angle

Digitalize the existing book. Lead with turning current offline buyers into online orders, not generic new-buyer traffic.

Rule

Every claim should be tied to proof and an ecosystem action: online order share, repeat transactions, tx fees, or buyer activation.
