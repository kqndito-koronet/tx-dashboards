## Activation Playbook by Pattern

### MAYESH Pattern (API + forward planning)
**Who:** Accounts with >15% digital AND forward online > $1M
**What works:** API integration lets buyers plan ahead. Forward availability published programmatically.
**Playbook:** Help them build/connect their web to Komet API. Forward inventory auto-publishes.
**Proof:** Mayesh = 35% forward online ($7.3M). The model scales.

### MAPLE Pattern (spot + high order volume)
**Who:** >15% digital, high order count, mostly spot
**What works:** Bunches + on-hand inventory listed online. Buyers browse and buy same-day.
**Playbook:** Maximize on-hand catalog online. Easy ordering UX. No forward complexity needed.
**Proof:** Maple Grove = 3,616 online orders vs 2,386 offline. Volume beats planning.

### ARIZONA Pattern (forward heavy, 0 online)
**Who:** >50% forward offline, $0 online
**What works:** Nothing yet — these accounts aren't online at all.
**Playbook:** Start with future availability publishing (prebooks/open market → visible online). Their buyers already plan ahead — give them a digital channel to do it.
**Blocker:** H4 from Maga — don't trust inventory counts. Need Impl team to set up.

### BRIDGE Pattern (some online, needs forward migration)
**Who:** Some online sell, >30% forward offline, forward not migrating
**What works:** Spot online works. Forward doesn't translate.
**Playbook:** Extend what works (spot) into forward. Publish prebook/open market inventory online. Train buyers to plan digitally.
**Key metric:** forward online % should converge toward forward offline %.

### SPOT Pattern (basic digital activation)
**Who:** Low forward offline, low/no online
**What works:** Mostly same-day business.
**Playbook:** Publish on-hand catalog, enable bunches, basic eShop activation. The Zeidler path.
