Layer 2 · Where Does the Ecosystem Grow?

L1 showed +3.1% GMV with K2K as the only growing channel. This dashboard breaks down by industry and corridor: who drives growth, who declines, who buys, who sells, and where do fees come from.
Importers (+11%, +$52M) mask Wholesaler decline (-2%, -$6M) and Grower collapse (-41%, -$20M).
  • Importers grow across every channel and generate 78% of fees ($889K). They are both the volume engine and the fee engine.
  • Wholesalers decline everywhere except K2K buy-side (+20%). Their fees ($211K) are 82% from BUYING via K2K, not from selling.
  • K2K fees are double-charged: the importer pays to SELL ($326K), the wholesaler pays to BUY ($172K). Same transaction, two fee events. This is good — both sides of the corridor pay.
  • Outside the Big 3 (Mayesh 24% online, KBC 14%, Bill Doran 3%), wholesalers are 99%+ offline.

Link 1: Farm → Importer ✓ Strong

Who buys: Importers buy $289M/H1 via Procurement from LatAm farms.

Top farm sellers: Rosaprima ($25M), EQR/Equatoroses ($22M), Ecoflowers ($6M), Fleuro ($5M), Expoflora ($5M).

Link 2: Importer → Wholesaler ✓ Working — fee engine

Who sells: Importers sell $26M via K2K (+49%) + $49M via eShop (+6%) + $30M via API (+10%) to wholesalers.

Who buys: Wholesalers buy $163M/H1 via Procurement. Top buyers use vendors like: Kennicott Central, BDC Mexico, Promoflor, Holex, EZ Flower, Queens Flower — all importers.

Fees (double-charged): Importer pays $326K (sell-side) + Wholesaler pays $172K (buy-side) = $498K from this link alone (44% of all fees).

K2K connections: Importers have 9,729 active selling connections. 191 accounts trade K2K. Broad participation, concentrated dollars (top 30 = 81%).

Link 3: Wholesaler → Retailer/Florist ✗ THE GAP

Who sells: Wholesalers sell $17M via eShop (-11%, declining) + $25M via API (-13%, declining). Only 3 wholesalers matter: KBC $6.3M, FloraLink $4.0M, Bill Doran $2.1M.

Who buys: Florists/retailers. We can't identify them by company (customer_id is local, doesn't map to company). This is a data gap.

Fees from wholesaler SELLING: Only $25K eCommerce + $12K API = $37K. Compare to $172K from wholesaler BUYING (K2K buy-side). Wholesalers generate 5x more fees by buying than by selling.

Offline: $249M at $0 fees. This IS the 82% offline block. Almost all wholesaler→retailer trade is phone/walk-in.

The story
We're good at Link 2 (importer→wholesaler): growing, fee-bearing, broad. The gap is Link 3 (wholesaler→retailer): declining, mostly offline, almost no fee revenue from selling. The play: help wholesalers digitalize their sales.
