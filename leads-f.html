Purpose

Units (loose bunches/stems from the cooler) are the dominant inventory format for wholesalers selling to florists. Yet most wholesalers list zero or near-zero Units online. This list identifies every wholesaler with meaningful Units sales and shows the gap between their offline Units volume and what they've listed online. The benchmarks at the top (Maple Grove at 35%, Mayesh at 21%, Zeidler at 17%) prove that listing Units online IS operationally feasible. The question is: why aren't the others doing it?
Methodology: SALE_DETAILS, H1 2026, ks_flag=TRUE, sales < $100K (Rule 4), company_industry = 'Floral - Wholesaler', inventory_division = 'Units'. Online = eCommerce + API + K2K (Rule 6). Offline = sales_channel = 'Offline'. Filtered to companies with total Units GMV > $100K. | Trust: trusted (all figures from SALE_DETAILS, standard filters applied).

Benchmarks -- Proof that Units CAN Sell Online

Maple Grove Floral -- 35.4% Units Online ($190K of $537K)

Sells Units through eCommerce to 55 buyers. This is a mid-size wholesaler proving the "live cooler" model works. 111 product categories online. Study their workflow.

Mayesh Wholesale Florist -- 20.6% Units Online ($6.6M of $32.3M)

The largest proof point. Sells Units online via API channel to 3,193 buyers. This is a scaled model -- API (their own website) handles most of it, not Komet eCommerce. The question: can other wholesalers replicate this with eShop instead of their own website?

Zeidler Floral -- 17.2% Units Online ($164K of $951K)

100% of Zeidler's online sales are Units. No Boxes at all. They sell loose stems via eCommerce to 35 buyers. This is the purest "live cooler to eShop" model. CS interview needed to understand their workflow.

Enablement Kit

WHY These Accounts (Strategic Logic)

  • H2 - Supply: 82% of GMV is offline at $0 fees. Most offline GMV is Units (bunches/stems). If Units aren't listed online, buyers CAN'T order digitally even if they want to. The supply gap is THE blocker for H3 (digitalize sales).
  • The math: $106.8M in Units sold offline across these 28 wholesalers. Even getting 5% of that online = $5.3M new online GMV. At blended K2K/eCom fee rates, that's $40-80K in new fees.
  • Prerequisite for H4 (acquisition): Don't drive traffic to an empty eShop. Listing Units = making the eShop worth visiting.

What to Ask (Discovery Script -- 5 questions)

  1. "You sell $X in loose bunches/stems every month by phone. How do your florists know what's available in your cooler right now?"
  2. "Do you have a way to track on-hand cooler inventory digitally today, or is it whiteboard/memory? How many SKUs are typically in the cooler at any time?"
  3. "If a florist could see your live cooler inventory on their phone and order for pickup or delivery, would that bring you new buyers or just move existing phone calls online?"
  4. "Zeidler Floral lists 14 product categories of loose stems online and sells to 35 buyers through eShop. What would it take for you to list even your top 5 products?"
  5. "What's the #1 reason you haven't listed bunches/stems online yet? Is it that you didn't know you could, or that the process seems too hard, or that you don't think your florists would use it?"

Expected Result Per Call

  • Best case: Wholesaler agrees to pilot listing top 5 products; Implementations schedules setup call within 2 weeks
  • Good case: Wholesaler identifies the operational blocker (no digital inventory tracking, staff training needed). CS logs it for Christine.
  • Discovery case: Learn whether the barrier is technical (can't do it), motivational (don't see value), or awareness (didn't know it was possible)
  • Metric: 3 wholesalers commit to bunch upload pilot in 30 days

Expected Objections

1. "My cooler inventory changes every hour -- I can't keep an online listing current"
Response: You don't need real-time. List what you receive in the morning, mark it as available, and it auto-depletes as orders come in. Maple Grove does this with 111 categories. We can start with just your top 5 products that are always in stock. If it sells out, the listing goes away automatically.
2. "My florists like calling -- they want to hear what's fresh today"
Response: The florists who call you today will probably keep calling. The opportunity is the florists who DON'T call -- the ones 2 hours away who can't visit your cooler. And the ones who order at 10pm when you're closed. Online listings don't replace the phone; they extend your hours and your reach.
3. "I don't have the staff to manage another system"
Response: One person, 15 minutes in the morning. Enter what you received, set the price, hit publish. That's it. Orders come in automatically. You're already entering this into Komet for invoicing -- we're just making it visible to buyers before they call. Zero new system, same Komet you already use.
