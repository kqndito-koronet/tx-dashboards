Deceleration Diagnosis: What is actually happening?

Hypothesis A: Online GMV declining?

REJECTED

Online GMV is UP every single week: +8.4%, +4.9%, +31.3%, +8.5% YoY. Total online GMV grew from $13.82M to $15.49M (+12.1%). GMV is not the problem.

Hypothesis B: Take rate declining?

REJECTED

Take rate is STABLE and HIGHER than 2025 every week. 2026: 0.863% → 0.871% → 0.873% → 0.866% (flat, ~0.87%). 2025: 0.652% → 0.662% → 0.688% → 0.676% (flat, ~0.67%). No deterioration in-week or week-over-week.

Hypothesis C: Comp effect?

THE ANSWER

The pacing % deceleration is purely a cumulative comp effect. W1 had a very soft 2025 comp ($21,352) — likely 4th of July holiday. W2 2025 was stronger ($25,653). The weekly YoY is STABLE: W1 +43%, W2 +38%, W3 +67% (soft W3 comp), W4 +39%. Underlying run rate = consistently ~+38-43% YoY.

Root cause of deceleration: arithmetic, not structural. Pacing % = (cumulative YoY) / (required YoY). As W1's holiday-inflated comp gets diluted by more normal weeks, the cumulative pacing naturally falls — then stabilizes. Every individual week ran at +38-43% YoY on both fees AND online GMV. Take rate held at ~0.87% all month. There is NO structural deceleration. The question for Aug/Sep: will the +38-43% rate sustain or accelerate toward the +54.5% target?

Is the deceleration caused by GMV or take rate? — Final answer

Online GMV growth YoY (2026 vs 2025)

GROWING +12.1%

Online GMV went from $13.82M to $15.49M. Every channel grew except API (which is volume-driven, not structural). K2K up +43.8%. eCommerce up +21.5%. No GMV contraction.

Take rate trend (2026 weekly vs 2025 weekly)

STABLE AND HIGHER

2026 weekly take rate: 0.863% → 0.871% → 0.873% → 0.866%. Essentially flat at ~0.87%. In 2025 it was 0.65–0.69%. The +20 bps improvement YoY is consistent. No channel mix shift is degrading the rate.

Conclusion: The +41.7% fee growth is driven by: (a) take rate expansion (+20 bps, accounts for ~65% of growth) + (b) online GMV growth (+12.1%, accounts for ~27% of growth) + (c) new accounts (+20-26 net new fee-paying accounts/week). The pacing "deceleration" is a comp effect, not structural. The structural risk is that Mayesh ($1.39M online GMV, $908 fees) and Rosaprima ($4.33M online GMV, $6,848 fees) are severely undermonetized — if those were at normal take rates, MTD fees would be ~$60K higher.
