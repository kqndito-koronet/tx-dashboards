L3b · The "Offline" Wholesalers — They're Not What You Think

These wholesalers appear 99% offline on the sell-side. But most of them BUY via Procurement, have active K2K connections, and some are ramping fast. The gap is sell-side digital, not platform adoption.
"Offline" is a misnomer. They BUY digital but SELL analog.
  • Most buy via Procurement — H&T ($2.4M), Tropical Blooms ($2.4M), Arizona Family ($2.3M), DKAY ($469K) all buy digitally from importers.
  • Most have active K2K connections — Southern Floral 288, Cleveland P&F 271, Baisch 244, Dreisbach 189. Lanes are wired.
  • Gap = connection→selling GMV. DKAY: 62 connections, $469K procurement buying, $0 online selling. They KNOW the system. They just don't sell through it.
  • New ramps prove it works: Flora Fresh ($0→$5.5M), WE GOT FLOWERS ($0→$2.9M), Stemline ($0→$2.3M), Sims ($126K→$1.25M).

The pattern

The irony
Arizona Family buys $2.34M via Procurement from importers but sells $0 online to its florists. They KNOW how to use digital tools for buying. The gap is 100% sell-side activation.

⚠️ Ninfa Flowers: H1'25 procurement shows $250M — this is anomalous (3x any other wholesaler). Likely a data quality issue. H1'26 value ($3.2M) is realistic. The H1'25 number should NOT be used for YoY comparison.

FloraLink is the proof of concept: $3.95M procurement buying AND $3.96M eCommerce selling. They do both. If Arizona Family, H&T, Tropical Blooms, and Ninfa did what FloraLink does, that's $10M+ in new online sell-side GMV.
