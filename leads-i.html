The Core Insight

"Offline" is a misnomer -- these wholesalers are already digital buyers

These 22 wholesalers manage $129.4M in Procurement purchases digitally through Komet. They browse vendor catalogs. They compare prices. They place orders electronically. They track purchase orders. They're NOT technology-averse. The gap is that nobody has helped them apply the SAME tools to their sell-side. The conversation is not "adopt technology" -- it's "flip the direction: if your vendors' catalog helped YOU buy better, your catalog can help your FLORISTS buy better."

Ecosystem data confirms: Procurement + Core users GROW online sales

From the ecosystem analysis (L3d): wholesalers with Core+Procurement grew eCommerce +23%. Core-only wholesalers went BACKWARDS (-44% eCom, +63% offline). Having Procurement doesn't cannibalize eCommerce -- it builds digital muscle that transfers to the sell-side. These 22 accounts have that muscle. They just need the nudge.

Enablement Kit

WHY These Accounts (Strategic Logic)

  • H1 - Connections: These wholesalers have procurement connections already active. The infrastructure exists on the buy-side. Extending to sell-side connections (K2K, eShop) is incremental, not greenfield.
  • H3 - Digitalize: The barrier is NOT technology adoption -- they already use Komet for procurement. The barrier is awareness (didn't know eShop exists), desire (don't see sell-side value), or ability (eShop is empty / not configured).
  • Fee impact: $103.4M offline at $0 fees. Even 5% conversion = $5.2M new online GMV. These accounts already generate K2K buy-side fees through procurement -- adding sell-side online creates the double-charge opportunity.

What to Ask (Discovery Script -- 5 questions)

  1. "You use Procurement to order from your vendors digitally -- that saves you time and gives you visibility into what's available. Have you thought about giving your florists the same experience?"
  2. "How do your florists currently see what you have in stock? Is it a phone call, a text, a printed list, or do they walk in?"
  3. "When you look at your vendor's catalog in Procurement, what's the one thing that makes it useful? Availability? Pricing? Photos? If we could give that same thing to your florists about YOUR inventory, would that be valuable?"
  4. "Do you have an eShop set up in Komet? (If yes: is it populated? If no: did you know it's included in your subscription?)"
  5. "If we helped you list your top 10 products on your eShop this week, and sent the link to 5 of your florists, what do you think would happen? Would they try it?"

Expected Result Per Call

  • Best case: Wholesaler agrees to eShop pilot. Christine schedules implementation. Target: eShop live with 10+ products in 2 weeks.
  • Good case: Wholesaler says "I didn't know I had an eShop." CS sends eShop link, schedules follow-up demo.
  • Discovery case: Learn the specific barrier. Is it "my florists won't use it"? "My inventory changes too fast"? "I don't have the time"? Log for pattern analysis.
  • Metric: 5 wholesalers agree to eShop pilot in 30 days. 2 go live within 60 days.

Expected Objections

1. "I use Procurement to buy, but selling online is different -- my florists like calling"
Response: Some of your florists will keep calling -- that's fine. But remember when you started using Procurement? You probably still called some vendors too. Over time, the digital channel grew because it was easier. Same thing will happen with your florists. Start small: list your top 10 items, send the link to 5 florists, see what happens.
2. "My inventory changes constantly -- I can't keep an online catalog current"
Response: Do your vendors keep THEIR catalog current in Procurement? Yes -- because they know if their catalog is stale, you'll buy from someone else. Same logic: if your eShop shows what's fresh today, your florists will check it every morning instead of calling. And Komet auto-depletes as you sell, so it stays current.
3. "I don't have the staff to manage another system"
Response: It's the same Komet you already use for Procurement and invoicing. One person, 15 minutes, morning routine: enter what's in the cooler, publish. Orders come through the same system you already use. Zero new software, zero new logins. The accounts in this list with Procurement already proved they can manage Komet -- this is one more tab, not one more system.
