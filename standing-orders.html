Standing Orders: The $142.5M at $0 Fees

Standing orders are the single largest fee-free volume concentration on the platform. $168.9M in total SO GMV, 84.4% of it offline and generating zero fees. There is no online standing order product.
$142.5M in standing orders generates $0 in transaction fees. But SOs and online CAN coexist (Mayesh, Sole Farms prove it).
  • Standing orders = 19.7% of all ecosystem GMV. One in five dollars flows through recurring offline orders.
  • 84.4% of SO GMV is offline — no digital channel, no fees. API captures 15.5% ($26.2M), everything else is negligible.
  • Importers dominate: 76% of SO GMV comes from importers (Rosaprima, EQR, Fresca Farms, etc.).
  • P1 connection migration: 270 of 686 P1 pairs (40%) moved from online to standing orders — $724K online became $10.9M offline.
  • Product gap: There is NO online standing order product. This is structural — not a sales problem.

The migration pattern
These 270 P1 pairs tried online first (total prior online GMV: $724K), then moved their recurring volume to standing orders. The result: $10.9M now flows offline, recurring, and fee-free.

This is not abandonment — it's optimization. Buyers found their preferred products and suppliers online, then locked in recurring orders through standing orders because there is no online SO product. They didn't leave digital because they disliked it. They left because the platform has no way to keep recurring orders online.
Product Gap: No Online Standing Order
$142.5M is structurally locked offline. There is no mechanism on the platform to place, manage, or fulfill standing orders through a digital channel. This is not a sales enablement problem or an adoption problem — it is a product gap.

An online SO product would:
• Capture fees on $142.5M of currently free volume
• Prevent P1 online-to-offline migration
• Coexist with existing channels (Sole Farms proves this)
• Reduce manual order entry for sellers

This is the single largest fee-free volume concentration in the platform.
