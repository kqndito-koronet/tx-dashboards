Definitions

Segment & Metric Definitions

Core + eShop
Wholesalers with Komet Core system AND at least one active eShop (buyer-facing storefront). These can sell via eCommerce, API, and K2K channels.
Core (online no eShop)
Wholesalers with Komet Core that sell online (via eCom, API, or K2K) but do NOT have an eShop. Includes major accounts like Mayesh (API-heavy), KBC, BDC. Note: API GMV counts as online but $25.1M API GMV from 3 accounts pays $0 in API fees (see FIX 3 sub-row).
Core (offline only)
Wholesalers with Komet Core that had ZERO online sell-side activity in the period. All their GMV flows through offline channels (phone, walk-in, standing orders entered manually).
K2K + eShop
Wholesalers connected via K2K (Komet-to-Komet network) that also have an eShop. 100% of their sell GMV is online (K2K is inherently digital).
eSuite
Wholesalers using the eSuite product (lightweight digital suite). Fee type = "Gross Profit Share" (distinct from eCom/API/K2K fees).
K2K only
Wholesalers connected via K2K network WITHOUT an eShop. Can sell and buy through K2K but do not have a buyer-facing storefront.
Procurement only
Wholesalers that ONLY buy through Koronet Procurement. They do not sell on the platform. They generate $0 direct fees but create INDIRECT fees on the vendor side.
Other / Unknown
Wholesalers in Koronet with no clear classification or minimal/no activity in the period. May include churned accounts or data quality issues.
"Our Platform GMV"
GMV transacted THROUGH Koronet platform channels. This is NOT their total business revenue -- it is only the portion flowing through our systems. A wholesaler doing $50M/year total may show $5M here if only 10% goes through Koronet.
"Online" (Sell Side)
eCom + API + K2K channels. Per channels.json (authoritative source). Excludes offline channels (phone, walk-in, standing orders entered manually).
"Online" (Buy Side)
Procurement channel + Web orders. Buying through the digital procurement system. Per channels.json.
"Indirect Fees" (K2K Commissions)
Fees generated on K2K transactions. The SELLER pays the K2K commission. When a buyer purchases through K2K, the vendor (seller) on the other side pays the fee. These are "indirect" from the buyer's perspective.
"Indirect Fees Generated by Buying (est @ 1.5%)"
ESTIMATE of the K2K fees that the buying activity of these wholesalers generates on the VENDOR side. Formula: Procurement Buy GMV x 1.5%. The 1.5% is a blended estimate -- actual rates vary by vendor (KBC vendors ~0.75%, standard ~1.5%). Could overstate by 30-50% for low-rate accounts. Per Data Rule 11: rates are per-account.

Est SFDC Revenue
When a prospect has no Koronet Core data, estimated revenue comes from SFDC Annual_Total_Sales field. This is their reported total business, not platform GMV.
Take Rate Range
0% (Mayesh API -- no fee on API channel) | 0.75% (KBC vendors) | 1.5% (standard K2K) | Skip (16 accounts with special arrangements). Actual fee rates are per-account, not uniform.
